# Comprehensive List of Australian Science and Research Prizes

This document provides a detailed list of prizes available to Australian scientists and researchers. It covers national government awards, state-based recognition, professional society medals, and private sector initiatives.

## 1. National Government Prizes

### Prime Minister's Prizes for Science
Australia's most prestigious awards for outstanding achievements in scientific research, research-based innovation, and excellence in science teaching.
*   **Prime Minister's Prize for Science:** For a significant advancement of knowledge through science.
*   **Prime Minister's Prize for Innovation:** For the innovative translation of scientific knowledge into a commercially available product, service, or process.
*   **Frank Fenner Prize for Life Scientist of the Year:** For early to mid-career researchers in life sciences.
*   **Malcolm McIntosh Prize for Physical Scientist of the Year:** For early to mid-career researchers in physical sciences.
*   **Prize for New Innovators:** For early achievement in commercializing scientific research.

## 2. State & Territory Government Prizes

Most Australian states and territories offer premier awards to recognize local scientific excellence.

### New South Wales
*   **NSW Premier's Prizes for Science & Engineering:** Recognizes excellence in science and engineering, with the top award being the "NSW Scientist of the Year". Categories include Innovation, Leadership, and Early Career research.

### Victoria
*   **Victoria Prize for Science and Innovation:** Administered by VESKI, this prize awards $50,000 to a senior scientist in Physical Sciences and another in Life Sciences.
*   **Victoria Fellowships:** Travel grants for early-career researchers to undertake short-term international study missions.

### Queensland
*   **Queensland Premier's Science Awards:** (Note: specific large-scale Premier's awards have varied, with focus often on the *Peter Doherty Awards for Excellence in STEM Education* and support for *Young Tall Poppy* awards).
*   **Peter Doherty Awards:** Recognizes students, teachers, support officers, schools, and education partners.

### Western Australia
*   **Premier's Science Awards:** Honours outstanding scientific research and engagement.
    *   **Scientist of the Year:** The premier award.
    *   **Woodside Early Career Scientist of the Year:** Promoting upcoming talent.
    *   **ExxonMobil Student Scientist of the Year:** For postgraduate students.

### South Australia
*   **SA Science Excellence and Innovation Awards:**
    *   **Scientist of the Year:** For outstanding leadership and research.
    *   **Innovator of the Year:** For translating research into economic or social outcomes.
    *   **PhD Research Excellence Award.**

### Tasmania
*   **Tasmanian STEM Excellence Awards:**
    *   **Premier's Tasmanian STEM Researcher of the Year.**
    *   **Minister's Tasmanian STEM Innovation of the Year.**
    *   **Tasmanian STEM Early Career Researcher of the Year.**

### Australian Capital Territory
*   **ACT Scientist of the Year Award:** An annual award for an early-career researcher (up to 10 years post-PhD) to act as a science ambassador for the territory. Prize includes $30,000.

### Northern Territory
*   **Radicle Science Awards NT:** Includes the **NT Young Tall Poppy Science Award** (in partnership with AIPS) and categories for STEM teachers and outreach.
*   **NT Science Week Awards:** Recognizes individuals and organizations promoting science.

## 3. Learned Academies & Professional Bodies

### Australian Academy of Science (AAS)
The AAS awards numerous honorific medals and lectureships.
*   **Macfarlane Burnet Medal and Lecture:** For biological sciences.
*   **Matthew Flinders Medal and Lecture:** For physical sciences.
*   **Dorothy Hill Medal:** For female researchers in Earth sciences (early-mid career).
*   **Gottschalk Medal:** For medical science research by early-career scientists.

### Australian Academy of Technology and Engineering (ATSE)
*   **Clunies Ross Awards:** Recognizes the application of science and technology for the economic, social, or environmental benefit of Australia.
*   **Batterham Medal:** For an early-career engineer who has achieved substantial peer/industry recognition.
*   **David & Valerie Solomon Award:** For an early-mid career science or technology graduate in industry/academia fostering collaboration.

### Australian Academy of Health and Medical Sciences (AAHMS)
*   **Jian Zhou Medal:** For a rising star in Australian health and medical science.
*   **Outstanding First Nations Researcher Medal:** For a mid-career Aboriginal and/or Torres Strait Islander researcher.

### Australian Institute of Physics (AIP)
*   **Walter Boas Medal:** For original research making an important contribution to physics in Australia.
*   **Bragg Gold Medal:** For the most outstanding PhD thesis in physics.
*   **TH Laby Medal:** For the most outstanding Honours/Masters thesis.

### Royal Australian Chemical Institute (RACI)
*   **Leighton Memorial Medal:** The Institute's most prestigious award for eminent services to chemistry.
*   **Rennie Memorial Medal:** For research excellence by an early-career chemist.
*   **Masson Memorial Award:** For a top-ranking undergraduate student likely to proceed to Honours.

## 4. Private Sector & Partnership Prizes

### Australian Museum Eureka Prizes
Known as the "Oscars of Australian science," these are awarded in partnership with government and private sponsors.
*   **Eureka Prize for Scientific Research:** Various categories (e.g., Infectious Diseases, Environmental Research).
*   **Eureka Prize for Innovation in Citizen Science.**
*   **Eureka Prize for Leadership in Science and Innovation.**
*   **Eureka Prize for Science Journalism/Communication.**

### L'Oréal-UNESCO For Women in Science
*   **Fellowships:** Awards $25,000 to outstanding early-career female scientists to support their research and career development.

### CSL (Commonwealth Serum Laboratories)
*   **CSL Florey Medal:** Honoring a researcher who has made significant achievements in biomedical science and human health advancement.

### Australian Mathematical Society (AustMS)
*   **Australian Mathematical Society Medal:** For distinguished research in the mathematical sciences.
*   **George Szekeres Medal:** For career achievement in mathematical sciences.
*   **Gavin Brown Prize:** For an outstanding and innovative piece of research in the mathematical sciences.
*   **Teaching Excellence Awards:** Improving learning and teaching in mathematics.
*   **WIMSIG Awards:** Including the **Cheryl E. Praeger Travel Awards** and **Anne Penfold Street Awards** for female students/ECRs, and the **Maryam Mirzakhani Award**.


## 5. Medical Research Institute Prizes (Internal & External)

### Walter and Eliza Hall Institute (WEHI)
*   **Burnet Prize:** WEHI's top science prize for early-career scientists with pioneering research.
*   **Colman Speed Honours Award:** For the top Honours student.

### Garvan Institute of Medical Research
*   While primarily a recipient of external awards, Garvan researchers are frequent winners of major state and national prizes, and the institute fosters excellence through internal recognition of high-impact papers and student achievements.
